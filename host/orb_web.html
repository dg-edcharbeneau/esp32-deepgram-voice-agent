Voice Orb Motion

The orb face of the round display, running live. Every dot comes from the board's own geometry code, orb_geometry.c, compiled to WebAssembly.

State

Switching state uses the board's 280 ms blend. Shell to shell blends ring by ring; anything to or from Thinking, Connecting or Buffering crossfades.

Voice
0.50

Only Listening and Speaking react. Listening scales to the microphone's measured range, up to 0.227 for a loud peak. Speaking scales to playback, up to 0.75.

Playback

Every state, live

Geometry is the firmware's own C, orb_geometry.c, compiled unchanged. The state-to-animation mapping, the 280 ms blends, the 90° turn while listening and the level smoothing (45 ms attack, 240 ms release) follow face_orb.c and ui.c. Dots are inked the way orb_raster.c inks them: brighter when nearer, scaled by the chosen colour.

The simulated speech is made up. On the board, the level and the three voice bands come from the microphone or the speaker. The other faces (clock, spectrum, touch panel) are not shown here. Rebuild this page with host/orb_web.sh.

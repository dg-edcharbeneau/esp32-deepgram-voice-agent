What this chart cannot see

Task stacks, driver and TLS buffers, and every heap allocation come out of that free figure once the firmware runs. A TLS session alone commonly holds tens of kilobytes for the life of a connection, and none of it appears here.

The number that settles it is free internal heap and largest free block on a live boot — esp_get_free_internal_heap_size() and heap_caps_get_largest_free_block(MALLOC_CAP_INTERNAL), which is where fragmentation shows up. That needs the board attached.
